its 30-day event window (T−30d, T] lies in a protected interval of its product. Price warm-ups are measured by running the real

(`close[t+5]/close[t]−1`). A feature series never spans a protected bar (recursive EMA/RSI/ATR would read it), so a series starts after the interval.

## 3. Pairing, models and costs

- Variants: **A** the frozen price features of the product class; **B** A plus every EVENT_FEATURES_V2 column of its sources (no selection, no dropped or added column;
  booleans 0/1, null item flags 0 and counted, `hours_since_last_new_*` capped at 720 with null → 720).
- A and B use **exactly the same decisions, labels, costs and splits**. A decision where a required source is not RESOLVED is excluded from both. A family's test set is the
  decisions common to all its products (so the bootstrap keeps cross-product dependence).
- Model: ridge, alpha 1, train-fitted standardization, one per product, for both variants (crypto: paper model v2 spec `paper_model_spec_hash` in the artifact; equity:
  `EquityModelSpecV1`). The fit is a refit on the forward train block (the split is new: the frozen 252-session walk-forward does not fit four months); training needs its own authorization.
- Reused frozen specs and cost model (crypto): `SIGNAL_SPEC_V1` `7f8b57f8…2939`, `RISK_SPEC_V1`, `ExecutionSpec` `99295b9a…93eb` (10 bps fee, 5 bps slippage, next-contiguous-bar open).
  No frozen equity execution or cost model exists, so **no equity economic metric is computed**.

## 4. Evaluation, decision rule and exclusions

- **Primary**: R = 1 − MSE_B / MSE_A on test decisions, per product, equal-weighted mean over a family (CRYPTO: BTC-USD, ETH-USD; EQUITY: the mapped instruments).
- **Uncertainty**: circular block bootstrap of the time-ordered paired decisions, 10,000 resamples, seed 20270801, blocks of 24 decisions (crypto) or 10 (equity), the same block starts
  for every product and both variants; percentile 2.5%–97.5%. Two co-primary families, each at one-sided 2.5% (family-wise one-sided 5%).
- **Minimum sample** (else no verdict): at least 10 blocks per product (240 crypto / 100 equity paired test decisions per product), at least 2 newly observed FOMC statements attested in the crypto test period,
  at least 20 newly observed EDGAR accessions in the equity test period.
- **Rule**: **SUPPORTED** if R > 0, the interval's lower bound > 0 and R ≥ 0.005. **NOT_SUPPORTED** if the sample suffices and not all are met (no evidence of improvement, not evidence of no effect).
  **INCONCLUSIVE_INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE** otherwise. Each family alone; no pooling; no other metric overturns it.
- **Secondary** (reported with the same bootstrap, never decisive): MAE, RMSE, Spearman rank IC, directional accuracy against the ZERO and TRAIN_MEAN baselines (both families); net return, Sharpe
  (8,760 periods/year), max drawdown, turnover, fees (crypto only).
- **No optional stopping**: fixed periods; no extension or re-run after a look.
- **Exclusions reported**, per product, split and variant, identically for A and B: protected interval or label/lookback/event window; price warm-up; event warm-up; source state not RESOLVED
  (per source and state); price gap or missing label; unknown or unverified mapping; integrity errors; null imputations; decisions kept.
