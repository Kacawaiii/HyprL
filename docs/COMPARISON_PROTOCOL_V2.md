## Why a revision

The operator's campaign mandate (2026-10-05) makes **crypto the primary objective** of the "prices only vs prices + events"
comparison, with its fixed criteria unchanged, and **equities an exploratory arm** of this first campaign. Revision 1 had two
co-primary families; it also stated in advance that the equity family would be INCONCLUSIVE at this period (37 test sessions per
instrument against the 100 a verdict needs). The change is made before any data exists, as a new revision with its own hash.

## What changes

| | Revision 1 | Revision 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | two co-primary families, CRYPTO and EQUITY | **CRYPTO primary** (BTC-USD, ETH-USD); EQUITY exploratory |
| Confirmatory families | 2, each at one-sided 2.5% (family-wise one-sided 5%) | 1: CRYPTO at one-sided 2.5%, the level revision 1 fixed for it, not relaxed; the equity arm spends no alpha |
| Equity verdict | SUPPORTED / NOT_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE by the same rule | **none**: R, its bootstrap interval (blocks of 10 sessions) and the secondary prediction metrics are reported as descriptive statistics, never cited as evidence for or against events |
| Equity minimum sample | 100 paired test sessions per instrument, 20 newly observed accessions | not a gate; both counts are reported |
| Effect of equities on crypto | separate families | none: the equity arm cannot change, delay or condition the crypto verdict |

The equity sessions looked at in this campaign become exploratory. A later confirmatory equity claim needs its own preregistered
revision on data not looked at; the reserved equity holdout (`equity_confirmatory_2027q1`, session dates 2026-12-01 → 2027-02-28)
stays closed.

## What does not change (checked by test, field by field)

- **Every crypto criterion**: primary metric R = 1 − MSE_B / MSE_A on test decisions, equal-weighted over BTC-USD and ETH-USD;
  circular block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, seed 20270801, blocks of 24 decisions, percentile 2.5%–97.5%, one-sided 2.5%;
  minimum 10 blocks (240 paired test decisions) per product and 2 newly observed FOMC statements in the test period;
  SUPPORTED if R > 0, the lower bound > 0 and R ≥ 0.005; NOT_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE_INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE as in revision 1.
- Calendar (capture 2027-03-02 → 2027-08-02, evaluation [2027-04-01, 2027-08-01), split 2027-06-01 with purge), protection
  table and admissible ranges, price and event warm-ups, admissibility, pairing, features (EVENT_FEATURES_V2), models, frozen
  signal / risk / execution specs and cost model, exclusions and their reporting, stopping rule, data, budgets and
  authorizations needed: identical to revision 1 (sections 1, 2, 3 and 5 of the [revision 1 document](COMPARISON_PROTOCOL_V1.md)).
